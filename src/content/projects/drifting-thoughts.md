A small iOS app for thoughts that are too short for a notes dump and too
personal for a social feed. You type something, pick a card style, and share
the image. Everything stays on the device.

The stack is SwiftUI and SwiftData. No backend, no sign-in, no sync. iOS 18
and Xcode 16. The Xcode project is generated with XcodeGen so the repo stays
a `project.yml` plus sources.
